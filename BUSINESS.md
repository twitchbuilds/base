# PurrBnB

**One-line pitch:** Rent a friendly, vet-checked cat by the hour — companionship on demand, zero litter box duty.

**Target customer:** Remote workers, travelers, and cat lovers in apartments that don't allow pets who want real feline company without a lifelong commitment.

**Features shipped:**
- Landing page with live marketplace of available cats
- Search + vibe filters (cuddly, playful, chill, chatty)
- Hourly booking flow with live price total and server-saved bookings
- Booking confirmation with reference code
- "List your cat" form that publishes a new host listing to the marketplace
- Live activity feed of recent rentals
- Pricing plans and testimonials
- Data persisted on the server in data/db.json
